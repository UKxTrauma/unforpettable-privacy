Privacy Policy for unforPettable

Effective Date: 21 November 2026 • Contact: petplannerauth@gmail.com

unforPettable ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our mobile application and how it is used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your display name, email address, and an encrypted (hashed) version of your password.
  • Pet & Care Schedule Data: Pet names, species, optional profile photos, optional medical/vet notes, scheduled care tasks, optional reminder times, and retroactive Touch Notes you enter.
  • Pet Sitter Sharing & Care Gallery Data: Email addresses and date ranges you explicitly share with another verified user, as well as care photos uploaded by an authorized pet sitter to a pet's shared gallery during active shared dates.
  • Notification Tokens: Device notification identifiers used solely to deliver scheduled pet-care reminder prompts to your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to sync your pet care schedules across your devices, send account verification and password reset codes, share selected dates and care gallery photos with your chosen pet sitter, deliver scheduled care reminder prompts, and respond to voluntary suggestions you submit.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or use third-party advertising trackers. Your data is securely processed using:

  • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted cloud database storage.
  • Render: Secure HTTPS cloud API hosting.
  • Brevo: Transactional email delivery for 6-digit account verification codes, password resets, and voluntary user suggestions.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivery of scheduled pet-care reminder prompts to your Android device.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated pets, profile and care gallery photos, tasks, and active shares at any time directly inside the app under Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. Care gallery photos associated with expired shares are also automatically purged from our servers.

If you have already uninstalled the app, you may also request full account or specific data deletion by emailing petplannerauth@gmail.com from your registered email address.

5. Children's Privacy

unforPettable is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
